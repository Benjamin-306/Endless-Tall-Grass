# GAME

<details>
<summary><b>View Info</b></summary>
<br>

Monpok is a game where you must find creatures called
monpoks and collect them.

But attention, you can only collect them if...

- you have enough space in your inventory and
- enough balls to catch them.

</details>

# CONTROLS

<details>
<summary><b>View Controls</b></summary>
<br>

Use <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>  or <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> to move your character around the world and explore new biomes!

Press <kbd>E</kbd> or the <kbd>I</kbd> button top left to open your inventory.

</details>
